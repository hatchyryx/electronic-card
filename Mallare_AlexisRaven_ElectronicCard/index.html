The Lost Codex

Welcome! Every journey begins with a single blueprint, but sometimes the best paths are the ones we redraw along the way. Step into a short, interactive narrative about shifting perspectives, finding new passions, and discovering where you truly belong.

HOW TO PLAY

Every meaningful journey is built one chapter at a time—from the heavy plates of early creative pursuits, to quiet moments of doubt, and finally to the bright logic of a brand-new path. Before you can unlock the final golden message, you must retrace these steps. Read each narrative carefully, discover the hidden truth within each story, and answer the checkpoint questions to guide the way forward.

Instructions:

  1. Read & Explore: Tap Chapter 1 to read the first part of the journey.
  2. Unlock the Path: Tap the next locked card and answer a quick question based on what you just read to open it.
  3. Keep Moving: Repeat this process to unlock Chapters 2, 3, and the Epilogue.
  4. Claim Your Reward: Complete all chapters to open the final Golden Envelope and reveal something special!
